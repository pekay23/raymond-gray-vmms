# Interim SOP content review — 19 November 2026

## Scope

Reviewed the four drafts in `docs/operations/sops/` against the owner-approved interim workshop operating policy and the user requirements for intake, pre-delivery inspection, routine maintenance, manual payment and handover. This review checks role order, record traceability, unsupported technical instructions and wording that could make a draft look effective.

## Findings and changes

- All four documents identify themselves as drafts, have no effective date and name the approvals still required.
- SOP-001 follows the approved handoff order: intake, Operations review, Team Lead planning, Supervisor task control, Technician updates, Supervisor completion, Team Lead release, Head inspection, then Service Advisor contact and handover.
- SOP-001 keeps the paying client, vehicle owner and visit contact separate. It also distinguishes customer reports from staff findings and source transcription.
- SOP-001 section 8 referred to a "current approved emergency-response SOP" without linking the approved interim escalation baseline. It now links the interim policy, points readers to the site's emergency plan and exact vehicle emergency guide, and states that the draft contains no isolation or rescue steps.
- SOP-002 labels its categories as a form structure. It requires the exact vehicle and market procedure, tools and safety rules before inspection. It does not provide measurements, torque values, HV actions or pass limits.
- SOP-003 labels 10,000 km or 12 months as an intake reminder, not a universal service interval. It requires the current model-specific maintenance schedule and leaves unknown baselines unknown.
- SOP-004 keeps invoice rates, tax, financial authority, email sending and receipt issuance subject to approved configuration. It does not treat a payment screenshot or showroom guarantee as settled payment by default.

## Decision

The drafts are suitable as workflow and recordkeeping references for further owner review. They are not approved technical, emergency, financial or legal procedures and must not be published as effective instructions or used to satisfy a VMMS gate. The controlled document owner, exact OEM procedures, inspection matrix, staff qualifications and grants, financial rules, email setup and site emergency plan remain pending.

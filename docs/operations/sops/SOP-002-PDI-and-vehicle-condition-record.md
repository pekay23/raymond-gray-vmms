# SOP-002 — Pre-delivery inspection and vehicle condition record

**Status:** DRAFT — technical owner and management approval required\
**Revision:** 0.1 (working draft)\
**Effective date:** None\
**Process owner / approver:** Pending appointment\
**Applies to:** Sales Representative, PDI Observer, assigned Technician / Engineer, Supervisor, Team Lead and Head of Service Centre\
**Related controls:** `REV-008`, `REV-018`, `REV-022`, `REV-037`

> This is a record structure and review sequence, not a vehicle-specific inspection instruction. Use the exact current manufacturer PDI checklist, service information, tooling and safety procedures approved for the vehicle model and market. Do not infer torque, clearance, fluid, battery, software, calibration or pass/fail values. No high-voltage task may be performed from this form.

## 1. Purpose

Record a vehicle's condition and required delivery checks before a sale or delivery, identify incomplete or failed items, assign follow-up work, and preserve the source and sign-off history. PDI observation does not prove current ownership or create service consent.

## 2. Applicability and start conditions

Create a PDI record linked to the specific sales delivery and verified vehicle. Capture VIN/chassis from the physical vehicle and compare it with source documents; retain the verification source and any mismatch. Record model, model year/variant, powertrain, registration where applicable, odometer and unit, inspection date, site, inspection stage, checklist template/revision and supporting manufacturer procedure revision. If any critical identifier is disputed, stop the PDI record from being finalized and refer the mismatch to the responsible Sales lead.

Before inspection, verify that the assigned inspector's role, competency, current qualification, required equipment and procedure are valid for each task. A general PDI Observer permission is not technical authority. Record safe custody, keys/accessories/documents received and any pre-existing damage with dated evidence. Do not start if safe access or required controls are unavailable.

## 3. Checklist completion rules

Each checklist item records: stable item ID and area; required procedure/source and revision; applicability; result (`pass`, `fail`, `not checked`, `not applicable`, `blocked`); actual observation or measured value with unit where required; reason/comment for every result other than pass; technician/inspector; date/time; evidence link where required; and reviewer decision. A blank item is not a pass. `Not applicable` requires a reason. A failed, blocked or unverified critical item prevents PDI completion until assigned and resolved or explicitly dispositioned under an approved policy.

## 4. Standard areas to map to the approved vehicle checklist

These areas organize the form only. The model-specific approved procedure supplies the actual inspection steps and criteria.

| Area | Capture on the form | Required basis before task can be performed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vehicle identity and delivery pack | VIN/chassis match, model/variant, records, manuals, keys and supplied items | Sales delivery document and vehicle identity procedure |
| Exterior and body | Panels, paint, glass, lamps, mirrors, trim, underbody/visible damage | Approved inspection method and damage recording rules |
| Wheels and tyres | Installed specification/condition, visible damage and any prescribed measurement | Model-specific limits, tools and torque instructions |
| Cabin, seating and restraints | Seat/trim condition, restraint indicators, doors, windows and controls | Manufacturer PDI steps; restraint-system work requires qualified authority |
| Driver controls and road equipment | Indicators, lamps, horn, wipers, washers and prescribed checks | Manufacturer steps and safe test conditions |
| Displays, warnings and software | Warning indicators, configuration/version observations and prescribed diagnostic results | Authorized diagnostic access, approved software procedure and data consent where required |
| Propulsion and energy system | External condition and approved status observations for the applicable EV, hybrid or ICE system | Model-specific procedures; high-voltage procedures and qualifications must be current and separately authorized |
| Charging / fuel interfaces | Port, cap, connector or interface condition and approved operation check | Exact model procedure and safe approved equipment; no energized HV intervention from this form |
| Leaks, fluids and visible service points | Condition or measurement only where the approved procedure specifies how and acceptance limits | Model-specific fluid, cleanliness, measurement and safe access instructions |
| Brakes, steering and suspension | Approved visual/functional inspections and prescribed measurements | Current manufacturer criteria, calibrated tooling and qualified task owner |
| Connectivity and convenience features | Applicable feature operation and customer-facing settings, with privacy-safe account use | Approved setup guide; no personal/workshop credentials left in the vehicle |
| Final cleanliness and presentation | Interior/exterior condition, removed protective material and loose-item check | Approved presentation standard |
| Outstanding items and delivery readiness | Unfinished work, safety/quality holds, assigned owner and disposition | Approved release and delivery criteria; unresolved critical items block completion |

## 5. Review and disposition

1. The inspector records each item and attaches evidence using the approved file flow.
2. The Supervisor reviews technical completeness, validity of procedure references and evidence, and assigns failed or incomplete work to eligible staff.
3. The Team Lead reviews completion and unresolved items. Corrections append a reasoned record; they do not remove the original observation.
4. The Head of Service Centre performs any separately required inspection and signs the applicable release record. No role may self-grant missing authority through a checklist.
5. Sales receives the delivery status only after the approved release criteria are met. A PDI record does not replace signed delivery, warranty activation or vehicle-owner verification.

## 6. Closeout report

The generated PDI report lists vehicle/delivery identity and evidence state; checklist revision and source procedures; completed, failed, incomplete, blocked and not-applicable counts; every item with result, actual observation/value, reason and comments; linked work/tasks and their current status; open holds; reviewer decisions; signers, role/grant snapshots and timestamps; correction history; and a clear overall state (`not started`, `in progress`, `blocked`, `requires recheck`, `complete`). Do not summarize incomplete items as passed. Do not declare delivery-ready while a release gate is open.

## 7. Approval required before use

The appointed Sales/PDI and technical owners must map this structure to current model/variant-specific manufacturer checklists, define items that require measurement or evidence, set competent signers and independent checks, identify critical failures, confirm site equipment and calibrations, agree document retention and publish effective revisions. Until those steps are approved, this draft is reference-only and cannot authorize PDI or delivery.

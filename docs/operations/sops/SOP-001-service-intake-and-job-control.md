# SOP-001 — Service intake and job control

**Status:** DRAFT — review and approval required before operational use\
**Revision:** 0.1 (working draft)\
**Effective date:** None\
**Process owner / approver:** Pending management appointment\
**Applies to:** Service Advisor, Operations Admin / Head of Service Centre, Team Lead, Supervisor, Technician / Engineer and supporting assigned roles\
**Related controls:** G1–G5; `REV-006`, `REV-010`, `REV-031`, `REV-046`, `REV-050`

> This draft describes the agreed handoffs and recordkeeping. It is not a technical repair, diagnostic, high-voltage, emergency-response or legal procedure. Do not use it as authority to perform work. The approved model-specific manufacturer instructions and current effective Raymond Gray procedures must be available before a task is released.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Create one traceable service record from a sales or customer contact through intake, review, assignment, work planning, task updates, completion, inspection, release, invoicing and vehicle handover. It applies to workshop visits and work originating in a service-request call. A request is not a work order, permission to diagnose, consent to repair, promise of a completion time, or approval to release a vehicle.

## 2. Responsibilities

| Role | Draft responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Service Advisor | Record the caller and visit contact, paying client, reported owner separately, vehicle and concerns; confirm the intake with the client; send authorized updates; prepare and check the invoice; coordinate collection or paid delivery after every release gate is satisfied. |
| Sales Representative | Record sales, delivery and PDI source facts within the approved role. Pass a customer concern to the Service Advisor or intake queue; do not create workshop authority by recording a sale. |
| Operations Admin / Head of Service Centre | Review new work, ensure scope and routing are clear, assign an eligible Team Lead, monitor all roles and reports, and perform the separately authorized inspection and sign-off. This role does not inherit VMMS Operator account-administration authority. |
| Team Lead | Prepare and revise the work plan; delegate tasks to a Supervisor; review reported progress; sign the final release and move the vehicle to the collection zone only after required checks pass. |
| Supervisor | Direct assigned technicians and engineers; ensure each assigned task is acknowledged and updated; record the technical completion decision and evidence or return incomplete work with a reason. |
| Technician / Engineer | Acknowledge assigned work, follow only current approved instructions within their verified competence, record findings/actions/evidence, and raise blockers or safety concerns promptly. An update does not sign off a job. |
| VMMS Operator | Manage accounts and approved role placements only. This role has no automatic technical, inspection, release, payment or policy authority. |

## 3. Intake and identity

1. Open or locate a service request, then confirm whether a work order already exists. Do not create a duplicate booking for the same visit.
2. Record the paying service client, vehicle owner and visit contact as separate relationships. Record unknown or unverified facts as unknown. Do not infer ownership, payer, consent or identity from a shared name, telephone number, email, registration mark or VIN alone.
3. Confirm the vehicle against its physical identifier and available vehicle documents. A scan may prefill a candidate; a staff member verifies it. Record powertrain as EV, HYB or ICE only after verification. Operations assigns a qualified Team Lead for a hybrid job until an approved routing policy exists.
4. Record the reported concern in the customer's own meaning, the date/time and source, current odometer and unit, vehicle condition evidence, requested contact method, visit contact and relationship, access/key custody, and whether the client requests a callback. Keep observations separate from diagnoses.
5. Record warranty as confirmed only when the required current evidence supports confirmation at booking. Otherwise record not confirmed or unknown. Never append the warranty marker to a booking reference on assumption.
6. Obtain and record the applicable client consent separately for intake, diagnosis, road test, data access, proposed work, and any additional work. A single general consent must not be reused for a different purpose. Diagnostic consent does not authorize repair.
7. Confirm what will happen next and whether the contact is a target callback or an approved commitment. Do not promise a repair completion time before the responsible role has reviewed the work.
8. If identity, consent, source, safety state, or required documentation cannot be confirmed, record the gap and route it for review. Keep the affected action blocked.

## 4. Review, assignment and work plan

1. The Operations Admin / Head of Service Centre reviews the new booking, reported scope, evidence and safety flags promptly. The review is a routing and completeness check; it does not approve a diagnosis, repair, technical procedure, price or release.
2. Assign an active Team Lead with a current qualification for the verified vehicle/work scope. Keep assignment, reason, actor and time in history. If no eligible lead exists, leave the job unassigned and escalate; do not override the qualification gate.
3. The Team Lead prepares a plan by splitting the authorized scope into clear tasks with an accountable Supervisor, eligible team, dependencies, required evidence, procedure reference and any customer approval required. A plan is coordination only and does not authorize work.
4. A Supervisor assigns work to named eligible technicians or engineers. Each person acknowledges, asks for clarification when needed, and updates progress against their own tasks. Keep each finding, test, action, follow-up, evidence item and correction attributable to its recorder.
5. Stop and raise a hold when the task exceeds approved scope, competence, equipment, evidence, consent, procedure or safety limits. Record the reason and who was notified. Only an authorized role may clear a hold under the approved procedure.

## 5. Work updates and customer contact

- Record a factual finding, test result, action taken, blocker or next step against the relevant task. Distinguish reported concern, reproduced/not reproduced, inconclusive result, completed action and pending work.
- Never use a customer's report or a historic document as proof that a current test was performed. Record the actual performer only when known and supported. A staff member transcribing a source report is identified as the transcriber, not as the original performer.
- Request scope, part, time, price or payer approval before proceeding whenever the approved policy requires it. Record the decision, evidence, decision maker and time. Silence is not approval.
- The Service Advisor shares a clear status, next step and callback time with the authorized visit contact or client using an approved channel. Record each attempt separately from a planned callback. Do not send owner or purchaser history to a contact without an approved purpose and recipient check.

## 6. Completion, inspection and release order

1. The Supervisor checks assigned work against the effective procedure, task evidence and applicable checklist. The Supervisor records completion only for work verified as complete; return an incomplete item with its reason and next action.
2. The Team Lead reviews task completion and required evidence. The Team Lead may return work for correction. Once requirements are met, the Team Lead signs final release to service and records placement in the collection zone. This is not permission to leave the site.
3. The Head of Service Centre performs the required inspection and records a separate sign-off. A hold, failed checklist item or missing authority blocks release. The release/inspection sequence and separate grants must be enforced by VMMS.
4. After the required sign-offs, the Service Advisor cleans the vehicle, completes the invoice checks, contacts the client and coordinates collection or a separately authorized paid delivery.
5. No vehicle leaves the site until payment is recorded as reconciled by an authorized person or a valid authorized showroom guarantee is recorded, safety/quality holds are clear, release is complete, and the handover recipient and vehicle condition are recorded.

## 7. Records and correction

Keep original entries, approvals, assignments, sign-offs, invoice revisions, payment evidence, communications and custody changes append-only. Correct a record by adding a new version with the reason and actor; do not overwrite the original. Link evidence by its accepted file identifier while keeping storage keys private. A source document does not itself prove truth, consent, authority or performance.

## 8. Escalation and stop-work

Anyone may stop their assigned activity and escalate an immediate hazard, unexpected condition, mismatch, suspected damage, wrong procedure, inadequate equipment, unclear instruction or work beyond competence. Follow the current approved emergency-response SOP. This draft contains no emergency isolation or rescue sequence. Preserve the vehicle and evidence, notify the Supervisor and responsible lead, record the event, and do not resume until the designated authority documents clearance.

## 9. Approval record required

Before this SOP can be effective, management must name the accountable process owner and approver; approve role boundaries and timing; reconcile the sequence with controlled quality, safety, finance and customer-contact procedures; specify retention and notification rules; and publish a signed revision with effective date, staff acknowledgement and supersession record. Until then, VMMS must label it draft and must not use it to satisfy a gate.

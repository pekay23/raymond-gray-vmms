# Interim workshop operating policy

**Policy ID:** RG-VMMS-POL-001  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** Owner-approved interim operating baseline  
**Approval source:** Owner instruction in this project conversation on 18 November 2026: “Treat recommendations as approved.”  
**Effective for workflow design:** 18 November 2026  
**Scope:** EV Afriq After Sales Service Centre, City Galleria; after-sales workshop administration and work control.

This policy records the owner's approval of reasonable interim operating recommendations. It is a source of workflow rules for VMMS implementation and pilot configuration. It does not claim to be a signed manufacturer procedure, qualification record, inspection certificate, financial delegation, or emergency response plan. The detailed SOP drafts remain drafts where they contain content that still needs exact OEM, technical, equipment, finance, or site evidence. A digital copy must be published through VMMS document control before staff rely on it as an in-app instruction.

## 1. Non-negotiable controls

1. Record the paying service client, reported vehicle owner, and person visiting or contacting the workshop as separate facts. Mark unverified facts as unknown and preserve their source.
2. Diagnostic consent does not authorize a repair. A plan, task assignment, call note, historical report, checklist draft, or vehicle-learning note does not grant permission to work.
3. Before repair work, the proposed work must identify the vehicle, exact work scope, expected charges where applicable, and the payer or a representative whose authority is checked. Record the decision and evidence. A revised scope needs a new approval. Silence and a callback request are not approval.
4. Use only the current procedure applicable to the verified make, model, variant, powertrain, market, and task. If it cannot be found or its revision/applicability cannot be confirmed, stop that task and ask the technical lead to resolve it. Do not substitute a generic EV, hybrid, ICE, or mileage checklist.
5. A role title alone does not prove current competence or grant technical, inspection, release, financial, or governance authority. Check the person's current assignment, applicable qualification evidence, and separate action grant at the time of the action.
6. Anyone may stop assigned work and report a hazard, mismatch, unexpected result, unsuitable tool, missing evidence, unclear instruction, or work beyond competence. Protect people, prevent further exposure where safe, notify the Supervisor and responsible Team Lead, preserve evidence, and resume only after an authorized person records clearance.
7. Do not improvise high-voltage isolation, rescue, battery handling, charging, or emergency steps. Use the current vehicle-specific manufacturer/emergency reference and trained, authorized personnel. If the correct instructions, training, equipment, or safe condition are absent, stop, secure the area from customer access, and escalate through the site's emergency process.
8. Keep records attributable and append-only. Record who did the work separately from who entered or transcribed a report. Correct by adding a reasoned revision; do not erase history. Keep customer details and evidence private to the authorized work purpose.

## 2. Workflow and role decisions

1. **Service Advisor or approved intake role:** capture the concern, payer, owner, visit contact, verified vehicle details, requested contact method, consent purpose, evidence, and next step. A service request is not a work order or work authority. Do not promise a completion time that the responsible workshop role has not confirmed.
2. **Head of Service Centre / Operations Admin:** review each new job for completeness, safety flags, consent, scope, evidence, and routing; assign an eligible Team Lead; monitor queues and reports; and resolve or delegate operational blockers. This role does not inherit VMMS Operator account-administration duties.
3. **Team Lead:** prepare a task-based plan within approved scope, identify procedure/source and competence needs, and hand work to a named Supervisor. The Team Lead may also perform technical work only when separately assigned, qualified, and authorized. The Team Lead does not use that technical participation to replace Supervisor completion or Head inspection.
4. **Supervisor:** assign work to eligible technicians/engineers, confirm acknowledgement, monitor updates, handle blockers, and verify task evidence. Only the assigned Supervisor with the current completion grant records technical completion. Incomplete, failed, not-checked, blocked, or not-applicable work must retain a clear reason and next action.
5. **Technician/Engineer:** acknowledge assigned work, follow the exact applicable instructions within verified competence, and record actual findings, measurements when prescribed, actions, evidence, and blockers. A worker update is not a job sign-off.
6. **Team Lead release:** after Supervisor completion, review the work and evidence, resolve holds, sign the distinct final release-to-service decision, and record movement to the collection zone. This does not authorize departure from the site.
7. **Head inspection:** perform and record the separate post-release inspection after Team Lead release. The Head decision neither substitutes for Supervisor completion nor Team Lead release. If the Head participated in the work being checked, assign an eligible independent checker; if none is available, keep the vehicle held and escalate.
8. **Service Advisor handover:** only after the required technical decisions and Head inspection are complete, ensure the vehicle is clean, check and issue the invoice under configured financial authority, then tell the client the verified status and collection/delivery next step.
9. **Vehicle departure:** no vehicle leaves until the release/inspection checks are clear and an authorized person records reconciled payment or a valid showroom guarantee, the receiving person's identity/authority, handover time, and vehicle condition. Generate a receipt only after payment is verified. A payment gateway is not part of this policy.
10. **VMMS Operator:** manage accounts, approved role placements, and access administration only. Do not grant this role technical or business sign-off merely because it is the top account-administration role.

## 3. Evidence and status rules

- Preserve source and time for customer-reported, staff-observed, measured, and externally verified facts; do not merge these categories.
- A completed result needs an attributable performer and the evidence required by the applicable procedure. Record values with the prescribed unit and instrument reference when the procedure requires measurement.
- Any result other than completed needs a plain-language reason. Not applicable also needs an applicability reason. Failed and blocked work needs an owner and next step before it can be considered resolved.
- Evidence must be linked to the correct organisation, site, vehicle, work order, task, and revision; accepted files must pass the configured scan before use. Never place private object keys or unnecessary personal information in reports or audit events.
- Unknown, unavailable, expired, conflicting, or unverified data remains visibly unknown and blocks decisions that depend on it. Do not infer a safe result from missing evidence.

## 4. Interim approval boundaries

This policy approves the role sequence, separation of authority, work-record standards, client scope-approval rule, stop-work/escalation rule, and release order above. It does not approve any model-specific maintenance interval or checklist item, HV procedure, repair method, numeric acceptance limit, calibration schedule, staff qualification, named person's sign-off grant, tax/price rule, payment limit, or emergency response sequence. These require exact controlled source material and configuration. The 10,000 km / 12-month reminder is a prompt to review the applicable vehicle schedule, not a universal maintenance package.

VMMS must implement these decisions as server-enforced gates with current grants and retained authority snapshots. If the required policy source, person qualification, evidence, consent, assignment, payment verification, or decision record is absent, the relevant action stays unavailable and explains what must happen next.

## 5. Review and change control

Review this interim policy at the first pilot review, after any relevant safety/quality incident, and whenever the owner supplies a superseding SOP or a manufacturer procedure changes. Preserve this revision and its approval source in the controlled document history. The policy owner remains the service-centre owner until a named document owner is configured.

## Reference sources used for the general safety boundary

- [UK Health and Safety Executive: electric and hybrid vehicles](https://www.hse.gov.uk/mvr/topics/electric-hybrid.htm) describes manufacturer differences, specialist skills/equipment, and hazards that may remain when a vehicle is switched off. Its general principles support stopping and consulting vehicle-specific instructions; it is not a Ghana-specific legal determination or a substitute for the manufacturer's procedure.
- [U.S. NHTSA: Emergency Response Guides](https://www.nhtsa.gov/emergency-response-guides) provides manufacturer-submitted, vehicle-specific emergency guides and rescue sheets for covered electric-powered vehicles. Use an exact applicable guide; an index page is not a service procedure.
- [U.S. Department of Energy AFDC: maintenance and safety of electric vehicles](https://afdc.energy.gov/vehicles/electric-maintenance) directs readers to manufacturer guidance and notes potential delayed ignition/reignition for damaged battery incidents. It is a general reference, not authority to perform repair or emergency response.

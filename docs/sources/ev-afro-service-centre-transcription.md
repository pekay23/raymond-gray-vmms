# Vehicle Type, Drivetrain & Systems Identification

Exercise 01 | Student handout | Suggested duration: 75 minutes | Individual or teams of 2–3

**Figure 1.** Vehicle-yard photograph supplied by the instructor. Select one vehicle to examine; the photo alone does not establish its EV type or drive layout.

### 1. Purpose and learning outcomes

Your assignment is to classify an assigned vehicle using evidence, identify its driven axle(s), and explain the location, purpose and interconnections of the principal systems covered in your foundation course.

- Distinguish BEV, PHEV, HEV, EREV/REEV and ICE classifications; explain any ambiguous hybrid architecture.
- Determine FWD, RWD or AWD and identify which device actually supplies torque to the road wheels.
- Locate or technically verify energy storage, propulsion, charging, low-voltage, thermal, braking, safety and body-control components.
- Sketch energy flow during driving, AC charging, DC charging and regenerative braking (where applicable).
- Separate observations, documentary/diagnostic confirmations and unresolved assumptions.

### 2. Safety and authorized scope

**STOP / DO NOT ATTEMPT.** No HV disconnection, insulation-resistance measurements, connector removal, underside access, jack/lift use, road testing, vehicle movement or live-circuit probing is included in this exercise. Use only observation, owner-facing interfaces and instructor-authorized diagnostic or documented information. If uncertain, ask before touching.

### 3. Execution schedule

Briefing 10 min | Visual assessment 15 min | Evidence-based checks 20 min | Diagram 15 min | Presentation 10 min | Submission 5 min.

## Page 4 — Energy flow and explanation

### 7. Part D — Draw and explain vehicle energy flow

Draw a clearly labelled block diagram of the assigned vehicle. Include components that the evidence supports; show the direction of energy flow with arrows. Cross out pathways that are not fitted.

- Traction: energy source → controller / power electronics → motor or engine → axle/wheels.
- Charging: AC path and/or DC fast-charging path; show which system controls charging.
- Regenerative braking: driven wheels → motor → inverter → battery (where supported).

### 8. Short technical explanation

1. What is the difference between an inverter, a DC/DC converter and an onboard charger?
2. If the vehicle has an engine and electric motor, what evidence distinguishes a PHEV from an EREV?
3. What evidence proves driven axle(s), rather than merely showing AWD badging?
4. Which systems might be fitted but hidden from normal view? How can you verify them safely?

## Notes for VMMS use

The source explicitly marks this as a student exercise and prohibits invasive/high-voltage work, road testing and vehicle movement within that exercise. Its safe, reusable learning patterns are evidence labels, confidence/assumption capture, uncertainty, system/architecture identification, energy-flow explanation and instructor review. It is not a complete technical knowledge base for every brand and model. Any added vehicle-specific facts require a named source, revision, scope and technical reviewer. Do not turn a worksheet answer into a diagnostic finding, repair task, qualification or procedure authority.

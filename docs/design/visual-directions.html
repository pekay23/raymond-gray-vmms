  • Any further artistic direction or newly created creative imagery.

Functional work can continue

Authentication, approved-owner permissions, accurate workflow states, responsive reflow, readable text, accessible controls and preservation of the approved image and logo. These do not authorise applying either proposed artistic direction.

Approval should name A, B, the current baseline, or specific amendments. Combining directions requires a concrete revised review.

Design rationale and evidence limits

The people and work. An advisor identifies a vehicle and records the customer's concern; an owner signs in to see permitted service information. The visual vocabulary comes from reception records, complaint lines, custody, task scope, evidence, independent inspection and handover.

The material palette. Existing logo blue and red, workshop charcoal, steel grey, paperwork white and neutral floor tones. Red remains restrained in the identity; safety meaning is always stated in text. Neither proposal introduces neon, gradients, invented performance numbers or a grid of decorative cards.

Purposeful structure. The ledger direction replaces a generic card stack with aligned record columns and explicit evidence labels. The editorial direction uses a strong reading sequence, a bounded photograph and quiet margin captions. Both preserve independent G1–G5 states and the difference between a draft and approval.

Assets. Local original logos are loaded from apps/web/public/brand. Every photograph uses the same existing apps/web/assets/sign-in/workshop-portrait.png. It is generated decorative imagery, not a photograph of a verified Raymond Gray facility. No new image was generated for this review.

Baseline. Reconstructed from apps/web/src/workspace.tsx and docs/architecture/interface-design.md. Its source sign-in uses username/password. The proposals show the requested Google, passkey and local fallback controls only as visual specimens. The operational excerpt contains no live records and proves no implemented workflow. Actual approved image variants and accessibility tests remain implementation work.
